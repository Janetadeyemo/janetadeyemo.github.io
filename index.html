Scales of Transformation — Self, Community, System

Janet Adeyemo is a population health leader, doctoral researcher, and certified personal transformation coach. For her entire career, she has dedicated her work to one question, asked from every possible direction: what does it truly take for a person to become well — in body, in circumstance, and in spirit? Whether guiding patients through a hospital system, building healthier neighborhoods, or coaching individuals through the quiet inner work of becoming, her mission remains singularly focused: transformation at every scale of a human life.

As Program Coordinator in the Department of Population Health at Boston Medical Center — New England's largest safety-net hospital — she serves as the designated lead for the MassHealth Community Initiative (MATCH) program, directing a team that helps clients navigate healthcare transitions and access housing, food, and transportation supports. Her work lives in the gap between services existing on paper and services actually reaching the people who need them. She closes that gap daily.

Janet is a Doctor of Health Sciences candidate at Bay Path University, where her research pursues the question her practice has already made urgent: why do health inequalities persist, even where healthcare is available? Her scholarship also examines the power of health promotion interventions in the management of complex chronic disease — exploring how education, prevention, and sustained behavior change can transform outcomes where treatment alone falls short. She holds both a Master of Science and Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Health Science from the University of Massachusetts Boston, grounding her leadership in rigorous health science. Her doctorate gives her coaching credibility; her coaching gives her research a soul; her clinical experience keeps both honest.

As a Certified Personal Transformation Coach, Janet has guided more than fifty clients through limiting beliefs, identity shifts, and career and life transitions — designing one-on-one and group programs in mindset mastery, emotional resilience, and goal execution, with a documented seventy percent client success rate. A Certified Masteries Coach with the International Association of Coaching and a Certified Global Visionary Leadership Coach, she believes transformation is never a hand-off. It is a relationship — a becoming — and she walks beside her clients through every step of it.

Her perspective is shaped by an international blend of culture and care. Bilingual in English and Yoruba, Janet brings a culturally grounded, deeply human presence to every space she enters — a hospital room, a neighborhood meeting, a coaching session. The settings change; the mission doesn't. When she is not coordinating care or coaching, she finds her clarity in the quiet — in stillness, reflection, and faith — believing that it is often in the silence that we finally hear what we are being called toward.
